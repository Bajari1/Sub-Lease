Physics-Driven Co-Op & Solo VR Liminal Furniture Heist & Vertical Extraction Sim
Target DemographicAges 15–30 (PCVR & Quest 3)
Core InspirationLethal Company × R.E.P.O. × Boneworks
Engine & PhysicsUnity 6 + PhysX 5 + OpenXR
Target Price$19.99 – $24.99
THE 10-SECOND HOOK: You and your crew breach a vertical maintenance hatch into the Backrooms for one reason: millions of dollars of untaxed, pristine Mid-Century Modern furniture, crystal chandeliers, arcade cabinets, and anomalous sculptures sitting down there for free. Rappel down vertical tubes into deeper liminal floors, steal heavy furniture, protect it from physics-driven monsters, and engineer pulleys, counterweights, and duct-tape contraptions to hoist a $3,500 haul back up a 100-foot shaft.

1. Your Loot IS Your Barricade

Every piece of furniture is both Money ($) and a Physics Tool. Wedging an $850 Mahogany Chair under a doorknob stops a monster—but every dent lowers its resale value!

2. Vertical Rappel Engineering

Sliding down a 100ft tube is easy. Getting a 220kg Marble Statue back up requires rigging 4x pulleys, dropping heavy counterweights, or inflating CO2 salvage bags.

3. 100% Universal Physics

Zero scripted lock-and-key puzzles. Flashlights, grappling hooks, ropes, and tape are physical rigidbodies that combine freely into traps, decoys, and vehicles.

1. The 6-Stage Core Gameplay Loop (15–25 Minute Runs)

1. Drop Hatch (0m)Check 3-Day Quota, buy ropes/tape/grapples, anchor primary lines.
2. Rappel TubesClip Waist Carabiner & descend vertical shafts (-30m to -600m).
3. Recon & ScanAppraise furniture ($/kg), scan for Mimics with UV light & scales.
4. Prep & RigTape caster wheels to sofas, wrap fragile loot, set tripwires.
5. Vertical HaulPulley-hoist or counterweight-lift loot up shafts under attack.
6. Cargo ScaleWeigh intact furniture & taped monsters, cash out, upgrade gear.
